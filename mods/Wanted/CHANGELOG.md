## 1.0.1
- Notices can be for any land you've opened, not only the newest two: half for the newest, a quarter for the one before it, and so on
  (a server setting, "Newest land share"). Older lands keep turning up late in the game.
- An idol reward is now of any land you've opened, each as likely, so older idols don't become rare as the game goes on.

## 1.0.0
- First release.
- Tested in single player; multiplayer and dedicated servers are untested.
- A craftable Wanted board with an iron-banded box: each dawn it posts notices for bounties, gathering, trophies and provisions,
  matched to how far the world has come.
- Look at a notice to read it; take it and it goes in your bag, tracking how far along you are.
- Kills count for everyone fighting within 30 m; a finished bounty gets a wax seal.
- Hand notices in at any board's box: the reward replaces them, with a coin pouch on the board while it waits.
- Rewards of coins and the land's materials, with now and then a useful bonus, a valuable, a rare find or a Forge idol.
- Finds other mods' creatures, things, food, meads, metals, arrows and valuables by itself; a contracts file to fine-tune.
- No board within 50 m of another; a new board gets its first notices at the next dawn.
- Settings in F1; the server decides them all.
- English, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Turkish and Chinese.
