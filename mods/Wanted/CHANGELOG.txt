1.0.1
- Notices can be for any land you've opened (half the newest, a quarter the one before, and so on: "Newest land share"), and an idol reward is of any opened land, each as likely, so older idols stay as common as new ones. Trophy notices priced at the kills a trophy takes at its real drop chance, asking mostly one or two. Wider spread of amounts asked. Illustrated notices (each player's choice, off by default): a sketch of the target in its kind's colour, tally marks and a heading (each kind's word in runes, WANTED in letters, or none), on the board and in the bag.

1.0.0
- First release: a craftable Wanted board posting bounty, gathering, trophy and provisions notices each dawn, matched to the world's progress; take a notice, do it, hand it in at any board's box for coins, the land's materials and now and then a bonus. Finds other mods' creatures and items by itself. Nine languages.
