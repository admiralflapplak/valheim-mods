## 1.0.0

- First release: Coglings, little copperwood robots that keep a base in order around their antenna.
- Work: floor items to the right chest, sorting by groups and re-sorting chests, repairing buildings and gear, tending fires, smelters and kilns.
- Breaks with a personality behind them: staring, dancing (others join in or clap), sitting on the ground or chairs, naps, push-ups, group chats, watching or following you, tag, sneaking up on another, tame animals, treasure, tinkering, looking over new building, sheltering from rain, fishing.
- Calling the Coglings from the antenna (E), waving back, welcoming you home, fear and cover, rebuilding after a breakdown.
- The antenna moves, creaks and has a lamp in the Coglings' eye colour.
- In English, Chinese, French, German, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Spanish and Turkish.
