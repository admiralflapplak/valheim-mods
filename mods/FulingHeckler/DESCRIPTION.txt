When you die, a Fuling runs over, laughs at you, and runs off. He keeps count of every way you've died, sometimes wears a hat or carries a torch, and acts out your death with the player emotes. He also turns up uninvited: lurking at the edge of your camp, creeping up while you're not looking, dancing on your grave, copying your emotes. On a server with the mod, everyone shares one goblin and sees him heckle each other.
